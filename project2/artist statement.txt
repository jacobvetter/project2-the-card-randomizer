I call this project “magic the gathering test hands.” The purpose of this project was to make my own sketch that I can upload names of cards into to run test hands on the fly to see if the deck is missing anything. I drew heavy inspiration from the rug outside of our classroom. I really tried to focus on the overlapping forms of shapes and colors that ebbed and flowed depending on the angle you looked at it. I made it to roughly the scale of how it would look on a table top. I picked the darker black and grey color scheme because I believe that shades of grey show off form and gateways into just everything better. I kept the cards white because I really wanted them to stand out from the background. I also did it that way because I wanted you to be able to read the text. I made use of animation throughout this piece as a way to help guide the eye to each card respectively. I made use of the defining variables to enable the text to actually show up on the cards. Getting text to show up was by far the hardest challenge in this project, only to realize it had a very very simple answer.


I picked the layout for the cards as I did, because when I place my cards out, I always do the top three cards then the bottom three cards, then I place the 7th and final card in the top row. I really wanted to showcase the habitual practice in this piece by heavily contrasting it with randomness.
